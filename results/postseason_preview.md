## 3. Stakes response

κ intercept and log g coefficient refitted on playoff challenges (σ_r and the other threshold terms fixed). Implied threshold slope = slope of logit(1 − p*) on log g with state controls, p* = calibrated belief at κ; a stake-rational rule has slope 1.

| side | κ log-g coef. regular | playoffs | implied slope regular | playoffs (regular params) | playoffs (refit) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Batter (called strikes) | -0.002 [-0.064, +0.060] | +0.101 [-0.255, +0.457] | 0.079 | 0.092 | 0.057 [-0.06, 0.18] |
| Catcher/pitcher (called balls) | +0.010 [-0.029, +0.050] | +0.042 [-0.167, +0.252] | 0.042 | 0.054 | 0.044 [-0.08, 0.17] |

**Playoff stakes.** Challenges do not carry over between games, so series win probability = P(series | lose) + L · P(win game), with L = P(series | win) − P(series | lose) fixed within a game (coin-flip series model). Maximising series WP within a game is therefore exactly maximising game WP: the regular-season game-WP DP is the right optimum in the playoffs, and the regular-season WP stakes understate playoff stakes only by a per-game factor L that rescales g and the token value Δ together. A stake-rational threshold therefore does not move with L; any response to L is a cross-game urgency effect.

- Series leverage L by round (min / median / max): Division Series 0.25/0.38/1.00; Wild Card 0.50/0.50/1.00; LCS 0.12/0.31/0.38.
- Refit on log(g·L) instead of log g: −log-lik 360.56 vs 358.37 (same parameter count); coefficient batter -0.147, catcher +0.008.
